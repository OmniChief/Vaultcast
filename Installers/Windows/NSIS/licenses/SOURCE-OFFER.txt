## Written offer

The source links above point at the exact upstream releases bundled in this
installer. If any link stops working, or if you prefer to receive the source from
us, then for at least three years after we last distributed this installer
version, OmniChief will give anyone who received the installer
the complete corresponding source code for the GPL-licensed components above.
That includes the source of the libraries built into the FFmpeg GPL build, on
a medium customarily used for software interchange, for no more than the cost
of physically performing the distribution. To ask for it, open an issue at
https://github.com/OmniChief/Vaultcast/issues titled "GPL source request" and
name the installer version.
